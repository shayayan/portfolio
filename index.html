Football Player Re-Identification Using Tactical Formations

DeepScouting • EPFL (Supervised by Claire Seceh, Alexandre Alahi, Selim Kebaier)

Confidential / Proprietary

Addressed limitations of appearance-based re-identification in wide-angle football videos by introducing a novel spatial approach:

  • Clustered players into tactical lines (defense, midfield, attack) using K-means clustering.
  • Assigned positional roles based on relative spatial organization within each line.
  • Achieved an overall accuracy of 62.1% and an IDF1 score of 76.62% on a private annotated dataset.
Note: The paper and code are confidential and proprietary property of DeepScouting.
